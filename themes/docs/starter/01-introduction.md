Welcome! These pages are a starting point for your documentation. Read them once, then edit or replace them with your own.

## How these docs are organized

Every page of your docs is a **page** in Bloogla (Admin → Pages). The sidebar is built from them automatically:

- The **`section`** custom field groups pages under a heading, like *Getting started* or *Guides*.
- The **`order`** custom field sets their order: 1, 2, 3...

Pages without a section are listed first, in order.
