Tell visitors what your channel is about, how often you publish, and where else they can find you.

**Adding videos:** create a post, press the **Video** button in the editor and paste a YouTube or Vimeo link. The video's thumbnail becomes the cover. Tags become the categories on the home page, and the `year` and `length` custom fields show on every video.
