A small roaster with big plans needed a brand that felt warm in a café and clear on a supermarket shelf.

We built the identity around a single mark: a cup seen from above, drawn with one line. It works stamped on paper bags, printed on cups and as a tiny app icon.

## What we made

- Logo and type system
- Bag and cup packaging for six coffees
- Signage for the first café
